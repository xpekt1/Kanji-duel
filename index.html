漢字デュエル Kanji Duel

  • Duel: a kanji is drawn, take turns saying a word that uses it (学校 or がっこう). Repeat a word or run out of time → lose ♥. Last one with ♥ wins.
  • Speed reading: everyone sees the same word and races to type its reading in hiragana. Faster = more points. 10 rounds.
